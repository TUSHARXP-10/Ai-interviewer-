    How the scoring works

    Every answer is analysed for what you said (does it cover what a strong answer includes, is it structured, specific, positive) and how you said it (fillers, hedging, pace, pauses, start delay, voice strength and tone). The interviewer combines these into seven skill scores, weighted for the role you chose. Speech recognition is done by your browser; all analysis runs locally on your device.

    Works best in Chrome or Edge with headphones in a quiet room. Other browsers use a free offline speech model that downloads once.
