    How the scoring works

    Every answer is analysed for what you said (does it cover what a strong answer includes, is it structured, specific, positive) and how you said it (fillers, hedging, pace, pauses, start delay, voice strength and tone). The interviewer combines these into seven skill scores, weighted for the role you chose. Speech recognition is done by your browser; all analysis runs locally on your device.

    Voice answers work best in Chrome or Edge with headphones in a quiet room. Typing mode works in any browser, even offline.

    Quick device check

    Takes 20 seconds. It makes sure the interviewer can hear you and you can hear the interviewer.

    1 · Can you hear the interviewer?

    Use headphones if you can, so the mic doesn't pick up the interviewer's voice.

    2 · Can the interviewer hear you?

    Say: "Hello, I am ready for my interview."

    Waiting for your voice…
    • Find a quiet place and speak at a normal pace, as if you were on a call.
    • The interviewer waits for you; take a breath before answering.
    • Press Enter or the Done button when you finish an answer.
